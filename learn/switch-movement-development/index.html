01CASI Framing

Current Level 3 Riding Module Course Guide：

Printed page 6

Medium Turns：

  • 6–10 m corridor
  • medium-radius sliding turns
  • forward + switch
  • advanced groomed slopes
  • intermediate speeds

Printed page 7

Medium criteria：

  • Core Riding Competencies in forward / switch
  • fluid linked movements
  • smooth forward-to-switch transition
  • pressure distributed throughout turn to achieve roundness

General Core Riding Competencies：

  • Centred & Mobile Position
  • Turning With the Lower Body
  • Balance Along the Working Edge

这些官方要求支持本课关注 Position、lower-body turning、working-edge relation、Pressure 与 roundness；下面的四-checkpoint development system 是本站课程模型。

02Four Checkpoints
1. Position
2. Lower-Body Steering
3. Edge Release
4. Pressure Distribution
03Checkpoint 1 — Position

Switch 常见问题：

body organization 仍按 Forward habitual direction 工作。

课程模型：

Switch Direction
↓
Functional Roles Reverse
↓
Old Habit Remains
↓
Position No Longer Matches Task

观察：

  • 是否 rigid
  • 是否保留 movement options
  • 是否 old leading-leg habit 仍主导
  • 是否 relative back seat
  • 是否能继续 release / steer / flex / extend

Centred 不等于固定 50/50。

04Checkpoint 2 — Lower-Body Steering

Switch 中视觉陌生容易出现：

Head Turns
↓
Shoulders Rotate
↓
Torso Leads
↓
Board Follows

课程目标：

Eyes / Head Look
+
Upper Body Organized
+
Lower Body Steers

观察：

  • nose / tail 是否共同参与 redirect
  • tail 是否突然扫出
  • torso 是否明显领先 board
  • current trailing foot 是否成为 kick source
05Trailing-Foot Sweep

必须按 current direction of travel 判断 trailing foot。

常见 upstream chain：

Hesitation
↓
Turn Starts Late
↓
Need Fast Direction Change
↓
Trailing Foot Kicks
↓
Board Gets Sideways
↓
Brake

因此不要只给“别踢后脚”的 correction；需要先检查 Timing / Release。

06Checkpoint 3 — Edge Release

课程模型：

Old Edge Releases
↓
Board Becomes Easier to Redirect
↓
Lower Body Steers
↓
New Edge Develops Progressively

如果 old edge 仍 high + loaded，而 rider 已尝试 Pivot，可能出现：

  • shoulder rotation
  • trailing-foot kick
  • abrupt steering

典型 Edge errors：

  • hold old edge too long
  • release then stay flat too long
  • grab new edge abruptly

目标是 timely release，不是越早越好。

07Checkpoint 4 — Pressure Distribution

CASI page 7 正式要求 pressure distributed throughout turn to achieve roundness。

本站解释：

throughout-turn pressure 不等于整弯主动向下踩。

需要避免：

Initiation
little happening

Execution
still waiting

Completion
big steering
+
big edge
+
big pressure

这通常表示 whole-turn development 太 late，而不是单独“Pressure 太大”。

08Integrated Chain
Centred & Mobile
↓
Old Edge Releases
↓
Lower Body Steers
↓
New Edge Develops
↓
Pressure Develops Through Turn
↓
Round Shape
↓
Smooth Release

动作之间真实会 overlap，不是机械按钮。

09Forward ↔ Switch Contrast

训练建议：

Forward
↓
Switch
↓
Forward
↓
Switch

尽量保持：

  • same terrain
  • similar speed
  • similar corridor
  • same intended outcome

Forward 是 reference condition。

连续刷 Switch 可能让新的 compensation 变熟，而不一定让技术原则真正 generalize。

10Comparison Matrix

比较：

  • roundness
  • rhythm
  • upper-body quietness
  • lower-body steering
  • old-edge release
  • new-edge build
  • pressure distribution
  • Heel / Toe symmetry

进一步做：

Forward Heel
Forward Toe
Switch Heel
Switch Toe

只用于 hypothesis generation。

11Training Ideas

Switch Traverse with Mobility

  • small flex / extend
  • small fore-aft adjustments
  • maintain edge support
  • verify body is not locked

Release Before Steering

只关注 old-edge release 与 board availability；不做机械停顿。

No Shoulder Rescue

避免 shoulder throw；做不出来就降低 terrain / speed。

Roundness First

优先 shape earlier，而不是追姿势美观。

Forward / Switch Matching

匹配：

  • turn size
  • roundness
  • speed
  • cadence

以上全部是本站训练设计。

14Teaching English

Stay mobile in your switch stance.

Release the old edge before you try to steer the board.

Let the lower body lead the turn.

Shape the turn earlier.

Don’t throw the shoulders into the turn.

Let the pressure build through the arc.

Match the rhythm of your forward turns.

18Failure Chain — Course Hypothesis
Unfamiliar → Wait → Late Movement → Rush
→ Trailing-Foot Kick / Upper-Body Rescue → Pressure Spike → Brake

这是课程分析／诊断假设，不是 CASI 官方 error taxonomy；late release 只是 kick 的可能成因。位置、陌生感、时机和动作习惯等变量也需检查，不能从单一症状确定唯一原因。动作会 overlap，Integrated Chain 不是固定离散序列；Pressure 随 board / rider / terrain / turn forces 被管理，不等于整弯主动下踩。

19学习路径

本站学习顺序：先理解 switch-riding 的角色反转，再明确 switch-medium-turns 的完整搓雪任务，然后用本页检查点发展动作。不声明 CASI 官方 prerequisite。
