01CASI Framing

当前项目对 Level 3 Riding Module Course Guide 的已审计记录支持：

Printed page 6

Medium Turns：

  • 6–10 m corridor
  • medium-radius sliding turns
  • forward + switch
  • advanced groomed terrain
  • intermediate speed

Printed page 7

Assessment criteria：

  • Core Riding Competencies in forward / switch
  • fluid linked movements
  • smooth transition from forward to switch
  • pressure throughout turn for roundness

当前核实资料没有规定唯一 Forward→Switch transition manoeuvre。

因此本页不把 specific 180 或固定 edge sequence 声明为唯一官方标准。

02Transition as a Bridge

课程模型：

Good Forward Riding
↓
Direction Changes
↓
Balance / Flow / Control Preserved
↓
Good Switch Riding

Transition 是 bridge，不是独立 trick。

03Entry / Conversion / Exit

本站分析模型：

Entry

  • balance stable?
  • speed appropriate?
  • last Forward turn still complete?
  • centred & mobile?

Conversion

  • board controlled?
  • panic edge / brake?
  • upper-body throw?
  • movement continuous?

Exit

  • arrive functional in Switch?
  • first Switch turn normal?
  • recovery turn needed?
  • rhythm restored?

不是 CASI 官方固定三阶段。

04Arrive Ready to Ride

关键问题：

Can you arrive in switch ready to ride?

而不仅是：

Can you get into switch?

05Preserve Movement Options

centred-and-mobile 贯穿 transition：

Before → Mobile
During → Mobile
After  → Mobile

不追求某个静态“正确姿势”。

06Over-Preparation

课程 hypothesis：

Prepare Too Early
↓
Stand / Flatten / Stop Shaping
↓
Last Forward Turn Loses Quality
↓
Transition

原则：

Do not let the transition eat the last Forward turn.

07Recovery Turn

如果：

Transition
↓
Huge Skid / Back Seat / Arms Swing
↓
Second Switch Turn Finally Normal

说明 direction changed，但 riding continuity 仍不完整。

课程目标：

First Switch turn should already be a normal turn.

08Timing & Coordination

timing-and-coordination 观察：

  • over-preparation
  • pause / reset
  • late reaction
  • cadence disruption
  • first Switch initiation timing

Smooth ≠ fast。

09Steering Versatility

steering-versatility 应让 rider 在 transition 后快速重新获得 intended Medium Sliding outcome。

Unexpected hook / carve 或 huge braking skid 都可以作为 clue。

10Training Ideas

课程训练设计：

3 + Transition + 3

3 Forward Medium → Transition → 3 Switch Medium

No Recovery Turn

第一 Switch turn 直接正常滑。

No Over-Preparation

最后一个 Forward turn 保持完整。

Pressure Continuity

Keep the legs working through the transition.

以上不是官方固定 progression。

11Video Analysis

推荐：

  • 2–3 Forward turns
  • transition
  • 3–4 Switch turns

第一遍只看：

  • shape
  • speed
  • skid
  • rhythm

第二遍再看：

  • position
  • release
  • shoulders
  • lower-body steering
  • pressure timing
12Teaching English

Keep the flow through the transition.

Don’t reset your body before you switch.

Finish the forward turn before you transition.

Arrive in switch ready for the next turn.

Keep the legs working through the transition.

16学习路径

本站先学习 switch-riding、switch-medium-turns 与 switch-movement-development，在两个方向能组织相同任务后，再练习衔接。这是本站学习顺序，不是 CASI 官方 prerequisite。
